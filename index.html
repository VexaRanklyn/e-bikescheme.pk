Available E-Bike Schemes in 2026

1. CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme 2026

Launched under the leadership of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, this scheme is designed primarily for university students residing in Punjab. The program is being rolled out in phases across major cities, starting with Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, and Bahawalpur.

  • Target Audience: University students in Punjab
  • Subsidy Type: Interest-free installment plan via Bank of Punjab
  • Application Method: Online ballot through bikes.punjab.gov.pk
  • Distribution: Phase-wise rollout based on demand and availability

2. PAVE Federal Electric Vehicle Program

The federal government's flagship initiative for promoting electric vehicles across Pakistan. While the program covers various EV categories, the e-bike segment has gained significant traction among middle-income households due to its affordability.

  • Target Audience: General public across all provinces
  • Coverage: Electric bikes, three-wheelers, and four-wheelers
  • Application Portal: pave.gov.pk
  • Eligibility: Income-based with broader age range

3. Punjab Teachers Foundation Bike Scheme

A specialized program by the Punjab Teachers Foundation (PTF) for government school teachers, allowing them to acquire bikes through favorable installment plans deducted directly from their monthly salary.

  • Target Audience: Government school teachers in Punjab
  • Payment Method: Salary deduction in easy installments
  • Application: Through ptf.punjab.gov.pk
  • Bike Options: Both electric and traditional fuel-based options available

Documents Required for Application

Standard documents required across most schemes:

  • Original CNIC and clear copy
  • Domicile certificate (for provincial schemes)
  • Educational certificates (for student schemes)
  • Salary certificate or income proof (where applicable)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Bank account details (preferably Bank of Punjab)
  • Utility bill as address proof
  • Mobile phone number registered in applicant's name

Step-by-Step Application Process

Phase 1: Pre-Application Preparation

Before starting the application, ensure you have all required documents scanned in clear, well-lit images. Many applications are rejected due to poor document quality. Read the eligibility criteria thoroughly to confirm you qualify for your chosen scheme.

Phase 2: Online Submission

Visit the official portal corresponding to your chosen scheme. Avoid third-party websites claiming to assist with applications — many are scams. The detailed walkthrough available at e-bikescheme.pk's application guide includes screenshots showing exactly what each form field looks like.

Phase 3: Document Verification

After submission, your documents undergo verification. This typically takes 7-14 working days. Keep your phone accessible during this period for verification calls.

Phase 4: Balloting (Where Applicable)

Due to overwhelming demand, schemes like the CM Punjab Scheme conduct transparent public balloting. Selected applicants are notified via SMS and through the official website.

Phase 5: Bank Financial Scrutiny

Selected candidates must complete Bank of Punjab's financial scrutiny process. This involves credit verification, down payment confirmation, and signing of the installment agreement.

Phase 6: Bike Collection

Once all formalities are complete, you'll receive instructions on collecting your e-bike from the designated dealer in your city. The entire process from application to collection typically takes 2-3 months.

Top Reasons for Application Rejection

  1. Incomplete documentation — Always double-check requirements
  2. Mismatched information across documents
  3. Poor quality scans that can't be verified
  4. Submitting after deadline — Apply early
  5. Not meeting eligibility criteria properly
  6. Multiple applications from the same person
  7. Failed bank scrutiny due to credit issues

Pro Tips for Application Success

  • Apply during the first week of the application window
  • Keep digital and physical copies of all documents
  • Verify your CNIC details match your educational records
  • Maintain a working phone number and active email
  • Avoid scam websites — only use official .gov.pk portals
  • Read all terms and conditions before signing
  • Bookmark the official site and check regularly for updates

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply to multiple schemes simultaneously?

Generally, no. Applying to multiple schemes can lead to disqualification from all. Choose the one most suitable for your profile.

Is there an application fee?

Official government schemes do not charge application fees. Any website asking for upfront payment is likely a scam.

How long does the entire process take?

From application to bike collection, expect approximately 2-3 months on average.

Can non-Punjab residents apply for the CM Punjab Scheme?

No, the CM Punjab Scheme requires Punjab domicile. However, the federal PAVE program is open to all Pakistani citizens.

What happens if I miss an installment?

Late payment penalties may apply, and continued default could result in bike repossession. Read the agreement carefully.
